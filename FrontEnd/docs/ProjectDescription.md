# Smart Rent & Decor — Project Description

## Overview

**Smart Rent & Decor** is an AI-powered mobile application that brings together house rental and interior design into a single, seamless platform. The app is built around a conversational chatbot interface, allowing users to browse rental properties, list their own homes, and visualize interior decoration ideas — all through natural, human-like interaction.

## What the App Does

The app serves two primary user types: **renters** looking for a place to live, and **landlords** wanting to list their properties.

Users have **two ways to explore** the app — they can either chat with the AI assistant or browse independently on their own. If a user prefers to skip the chatbot entirely, they can jump straight into the listings, apply filters manually, and navigate the app just like a traditional property platform. Both modes are fully supported, and users can switch between them anytime.

For those who prefer the chatbot, a renter can simply type something like _"Show me 2-bedroom houses in Lahore under 50,000 PKR"_ and instantly receive tailored listings. For those who prefer to self-browse, they can scroll through listings, apply filters by location, price, bedrooms, and amenities, and explore at their own pace — no conversation required.

For landlords, the process is equally simple — they upload property photos, fill in rental details, and their listing goes live for potential tenants to discover.

## The AI Decoration Feature

Beyond rentals, the app includes a standout feature: **AI-powered interior design**. Users can upload a photo of any room, and the app sends it to an AI image generation model that returns redesigned versions with modern interior ideas. This helps users visualize and plan how a space could look before committing to any changes — a powerful tool whether you're moving into a new home or refreshing your current one.

## User Experience Goals

The app offers two distinct but equally polished modes of interaction. Users who enjoy guided, conversational experiences can rely on the AI chatbot, while users who prefer to be in full control can browse freely on their own. Neither mode feels like a compromise — both are first-class experiences. The app should feel intuitive whether a user types their first question or taps straight into the listings.

Notifications keep users updated on property availability and messages from owners regardless of which mode they use.

## Core Screens to Build

The frontend will consist of the following key screens and flows:

- **Onboarding / Auth** — Sign up and login with email or Google
- **Home / Mode Selection** — Entry point where users choose to chat with the AI or browse on their own
- **Chatbot Interface** — Conversational AI assistant that guides users through finding properties
- **Browse Listings** — Independent grid/list view of all available properties with images and key details, accessible without the chatbot
- **Search & Filters** — Manual filters for location, price range, bedroom count, and amenities for self-browsing users
- **Property Detail** — Full property page with photos, price, description, and contact option
- **Post a Property** — Form for landlords to upload and publish their listings
- **AI Decoration Studio** — Upload a room photo and view AI-generated redesign results
- **Notifications** — Updates on messages and property status
